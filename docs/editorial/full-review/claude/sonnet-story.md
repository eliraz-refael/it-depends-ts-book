## 6. Overall readiness verdict

**Ready after fixes.** The stories are the book's strength: nearly every Act 3-4 chapter has a believable bug, a Turn that really turns (Ch7, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 23, 25, 26, B1-B3), and a Verdict earned by what was shown, and the cast behaves like the bios in the big moments (Noam asks for the red run; Idan wants the produced value; Guy adapts when pushed; Dafna concedes to a loop; Chen asks the unanswerable question). What would hurt in print is structural and mechanical, not narrative: six consecutive chapters (8-13) with no stated Principle, a dozen Verdicts that are meeting minutes rather than rulings, a cluster of out-of-fiction narrator lines ("In these snapshots", "fictional shop", "Keep the actual condition attached"), a repeated silent-closer tic, and the almost complete absence of the bios' catchphrases. Fix those and tighten a few over-built chapters (19, 21, 26, 27) and the book is ready; no chapter needs its story rebuilt.
