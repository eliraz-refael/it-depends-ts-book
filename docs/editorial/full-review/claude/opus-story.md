## 6. Overall readiness verdict

**Ready after fixes.** The stories hold up. The Principle contract is kept in Part 1, dropped in Part 2, and in Parts 3–5 swells into manifestos that give away the Turn. Verdict format drifts too. The "leaves X beside Y" closers, the narrator's spec paragraphs and the two "fictional shop" lines are exactly the AI tells the brief warns about. The cast page's catchphrases almost never show up. All of these are editing passes, not rewrites: trim the Principles, state the rule, cut the closers, fix the `RequestState` drift and the missing Ch10 Turn, and add the few missing callbacks.
