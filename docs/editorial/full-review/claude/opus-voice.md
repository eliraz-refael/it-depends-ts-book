## 6. Overall readiness verdict

**Ready after fixes.** The book is genuinely fun at its peaks — Ch1, Ch7, Ch17, Ch23 and all three Bonus chapters have lines a reader will quote, and the technical spine is careful. But the voice is not yet one book. It drifts in three visible phases: Act I's short Principles and blockquote Verdicts; an Act II slump (Ch8-13) with no stated rules, PR-minute Verdicts and a narrator who keeps ending scenes on someone silently placing a file "beside" another; and Acts III-V whose Principles give away the twist and whose narrator reads out configs and diagnostics like a CI log. Two "fictional shop" lines and three other frame leaks (the book, Chapter 13) must go before print. The cast is underused against its own bios — catchphrases almost never spoken, Dima and Sahar barely present, Chen and Gil drifting — and the late-book humour converges on one deadpan. None of this needs new chapters; it needs a voice pass using Ch7 as the tuning fork, a rule-only first paragraph for every Principle, a ruling-first Verdict for every chapter, and an ending.
