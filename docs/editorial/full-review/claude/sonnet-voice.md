## 6. Overall readiness verdict
**Ready after fixes.** The book is better than its register suggests: characters are cast with real comic instincts (Oded, Noam, Gilad, Dafna, Idan especially), the cold opens from Ch12 on are excellent, and the callbacks across 30 chapters hold. What is not print-ready is the voice discipline. Roughly a third of the lines I'd flag as AI-sounding are structural and fixable in a mechanical pass: ~250-word Principle monologues, narrator paragraphs that pre-empt nit-picks, 36 docs hyperlinks inside the story, 22 "leaves X beside Y" closers, "in the review" endings, and four fourth-wall slips ("fictional shop" x2, "the book's profile" x2). The deeper problem is that the cast page's catchphrases and the How-to-Read skeleton (an expert states a testable rule up front; a Verdict rules or admits it depends) are kept in about half the book; Ch8-13 lose the Principle rule and Ch8-16 slide toward PR-status Verdicts. None of this requires re-plotting. A focused edit that (1) trims Principles to one rule plus one image, (2) strips links and process asides, (3) replaces silent closers and report Verdicts with dialogue, and (4) puts the catchphrases back would bring Acts 2-3 up to Ch7/Ch13/Bonus 1 quality and make the final act's strength the norm rather than the exception.
